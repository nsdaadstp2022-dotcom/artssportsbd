ABOUT ARTS FASHION BD

Top quality sportswear and custom jerseys, made in Bangladesh

Arts Fashion BD is a Gazipur-based sportswear and custom jersey brand, built around one idea: everyone deserves a jersey that feels like it's actually theirs. From national team editions to fully personalised jerseys with your name, number and team logo, every piece is made with premium sublimation printing and durable fabric that holds up wash after wash.

We work with individuals, school teams, and corporate groups across Bangladesh — handling everything from single custom orders to bulk team kits, with nationwide delivery and cash-on-delivery available.

Premium materials

Breathable polyester and cotton-blend fabrics chosen for comfort, durability, and print quality.

Full customisation

Name, number, and team logo printing via digital sublimation — built for individuals and full team orders.

Nationwide delivery

Fast delivery across Bangladesh, with free shipping inside Dhaka on orders above ৳2,000.
